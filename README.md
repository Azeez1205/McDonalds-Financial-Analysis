# 🍔 McDonalds Corp. 3 Statement Modelling & DCF Analysis

## 📝 Overview
This project offers McDonalds Corp. a fully integrated three-statement financial model in order to evaluate past performance and project financial results over a five-year period.

To comprehend how operational assumptions affect profitability, liquidity, and overall financial sustainability, the model connects the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement.

A Discounted Cash Flow analysis was also performed to estimate McDonalds Corp's intrinsic enterprise and equity value, incorporating a Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) calculation and a terminal value based on the perpetuity growth method.

## 🎯 Objectives
### The purpose of this model is to practice and demonstrate financial modeling skills, including:

- Building a statement of Profit or loss, Financial position and Cash flows forecast.
- Understanding financial statement linkages and accounting flow.
- Forecasting operating performance using realistic assumptions.
- Creating a foundation for valuation and investment analysis.
- Deriving Unlevered Free Cash Flows from the integrated financial statements.
- Calculating a company-specific WACC using CAPM and a weighted-average cost of debt.
- Applying a DCF valuation to arrive at an implied enterprise and equity value.

## 💫 Model Includes
- Historical and forecasted profit or statement
- Historical and forecasted statement of financial position
- Historical and forecasted cash flow statement
- Discounted Cash flow Analysis
- Weighted average cost of capital calculation
- Terminal Value calculation(Accounting for the present value)
- Implied Value per share

## 🔭 Key Forecast Assumptions
### The forecast is based on historical averages and model assumptions, including but not limited to:

- Revenue growth
- Cost of goods sold as a percentage of revenue
- Depreciation and amortization as a percentage of PP&E
- Tax rate
- Capital expenditures
- Debt issuance and repayment
- Equity issuance and repurchases

## 🛠️ Skills Demonstrated
- Excel financial modeling
- 3-statement modeling
- Forecasting
- Financial statement analysis
- Formula linking and auditing

## Notes
This model was created for learning and portfolio purposes. Forecasts are based on simplified assumptions and should not be interpreted as investment advice.

The model remains under active review. Errors identified through ongoing testing will be corrected as further refinements are made.
